THE MECHANIC

HOW YOU PLAY

Before every snap, you draw your run lane directly on the field — tracing the path you want your running back to take through the defense. Then you hike. The RB follows your line, defenders converge, and everything plays out in real time based on your route choice, blocker placement, and step type selection.

The step economy is the core system: every segment of your drawn route costs steps based on the step type (Normal, Speed, Juke, Spin). Running backs have a maximum step pool. Spend wisely. Spend wrong and you run out of moves mid-field.

GAME SYSTEMS

WHAT WAS BUILT

✏️
LANE DRAWING
Drag-to-draw route system on a live scrollable field. Step dots placed along the route show type and cost. Clear and redraw any time before the snap.
⚡
4 STEP TYPES
Normal (1), Speed (2-4), Juke (2-5), Spin (2-6) — costs vary by RB. Speed triggers a 2.2x burst. Juke redirects nearby defenders. Spin creates confusion radius.
🛡️
BLOCKER PLACEMENT
Pre-snap blocker tiles placed on the field. Nearest blitzing defender gets blocked. Costs steps from your pool — a real strategic tradeoff before every snap.
🌀
SWIPE JUKE
During the run, swipe left or right to trigger a live juke. Costs 3 steps. Success rate depends on the RB's juke rating. Fail and the nearest defender reads it.
🏃
6 RUNNING BACKS
Rookie, The Burner, The Juker, The Spinner, Weapon X, The Legend — each with distinct step costs, success rates, and big run multipliers. Unlock with VC earned in-game.
⬆️
UPGRADE ECONOMY
Spend VC to improve max steps, juke success rate, broken tackle rate, big run multiplier, blocker cost, and individual step costs. Full locker room upgrade system.
🏟️
DEFENDER AI
6 defenders with distinct roles (LB, CB, S). Blitz detection pre-snap. Commitment timer system — defenders over-pursue then recover. Real physics-based pursuit.
📅
SEASON MODE
10-game season against named opponents. Career manager with your city and team name. Week-by-week schedule. Post-game results. Win-loss record tracking.
