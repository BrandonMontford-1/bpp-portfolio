THE ROAD TO APP STORE

REJECTED. RESUBMITTED. CLEARED.

RouteRunner's first App Store submission was rejected — not for the game itself, but for a missing screen recording during review. Standard information request. The fix was straightforward: record the gameplay, document the features, resubmit. Cleared May 26, 2026.

That rejection-to-approval arc is part of the story. Shipping is a process, not a single moment. RouteRunner went through it and came out the other side live on the App Store with all three football games now active or in progress.

GAME ENGINE

WHAT RUNS UNDER THE HOOD

RouteRunner is built on a custom SwiftUI game engine with real-time physics, separation tracking, and defender AI. Every game system was built from scratch.

🗺️
8 ROUTE TYPES
Slant, post, corner, go, curl, out, dig — plus a freehand Draw route. Each route has distinct timing windows and defender reactions.
📐
SEPARATION TRACKING
Real-time distance between WR and CB calculated every frame. Separation bar updates live. Open window triggers visual and haptic feedback.
🏆
QB GRADE SYSTEM
Every throw is graded A+ through F based on separation at release, yards gained, first downs, touchdowns. Grade persists across the game.
🛡️
3 ROTATING DEFENDERS
Hall of Fame, All-Pro, Pro Bowler — each with escalating speed, coverage lag, and reaction time. Defenders rotate mid-game after 8 plays.
💬
CB TRASH TALK
Context-triggered trash talk based on coverage tightness, drops, interceptions, and touchdowns. Over 20 unique lines across 6 trigger types.
📅
10-WEEK CAREER SEASON
Full schedule against 10 teams. Per-game stats logging, season standings, passer rating calculation, post-game and season-end screens.
