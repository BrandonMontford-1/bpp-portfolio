ORIGIN

YEARS BEFORE THE CODE

The Miracle League of South Fulton provides individuals with disabilities the opportunity to play organized baseball in a non-competitive, joyful atmosphere — with volunteer buddies on the field every step of the way. I volunteered with this league for years before I ever wrote a line of code for them.

When I started building mobile apps, I knew exactly what they needed. Not a generic community app — something built around how they actually operate. Buddy assignments, emergency access on the field, game schedules, registration with the information families actually need to provide. Everything in the app came from watching it work in person.

FEATURES

WHAT WAS BUILT

📅
SEASON SCHEDULE
Full Spring 2026 schedule with game dates, locations, times, and attire. Banquet details with ticket pricing. Always current, always accessible.
📝
REGISTRATION FLOW
New player, returning player, and volunteer registration — each with the right fields. Emergency contact collection, medical notes, parent/guardian info all built in.
🤝
BUDDY SYSTEM
Volunteer buddies can record their assigned player, access field tips for Angels in the Outfield, and tap an emergency SOS modal for immediate help.
🚨
EMERGENCY SOS
One tap from the Buddy tab triggers an emergency overlay with direct 911 calling and buddy alert. Built for real field conditions where seconds matter.
📸
PHOTO GALLERY
Masonry-style game day photo gallery with camera roll integration. Each slot can be filled with real photos from families and volunteers.
📞
CONTACT & COMMS
Direct tap-to-call and tap-to-email for Karen Nunnally (President) and the communications team. Directions to Burdett Park. Message form built in.
CLIENT

THE ORGANIZATION

The Miracle League of South Fulton is a registered nonprofit based in Red Oak, GA. Their mission: provide individuals with disabilities the opportunity to play organized baseball in a non-competitive, joyful atmosphere. Every player gets a volunteer buddy — an Angel in the Outfield — on the field with them every inning.

The app serves players, families, volunteers, and staff. It's not a promotional tool — it's operational infrastructure for a community that runs on coordination and care.
