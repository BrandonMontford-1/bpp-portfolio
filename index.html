Our Mission

Joy on
Every Base

The Miracle League of South Fulton is a non-profit organization providing individuals with disabilities the opportunity to play organized baseball in a non-competitive, joyful atmosphere.

Every player is paired with a volunteer Angel in the Outfield—a dedicated buddy who stays by their side every inning, helping them bat, run the bases, and celebrate every play.

Every player gets a dedicated buddy every inning. Here's how to be a great one.

🚨
Emergency Hotline
Call 678-870-6306 · immediate field updates
⚾ Angel in the Outfield Tips
✦ Stay close
Stay within arm's reach of your player on the field at all times.
✦ Help with batting
Assist your player gripping and swinging the bat safely and confidently.
✦ Run the bases together
Guide your player around the bases with energy and loud cheers.
✦ Celebrate everything
Every play is a win—cheer like it's the World Series.
✦ Follow their lead
Let the player set the pace. Support without rushing or redirecting.
🤝 Become a Buddy

Volunteer buddies make the Miracle League possible. No experience necessary—just a big heart and a love of baseball. We'll pair you with a player and walk you through everything before game day.
